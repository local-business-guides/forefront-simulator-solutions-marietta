What to ask and prepare before you visit or call

A short checklist can make the first conversation more productive:

  • Write down the golf-related product or service you want to ask about.
  • If you’re considering a simulator, note whether the project is residential or commercial.
  • Think about how many people may use the setup and whether practice, play, or both matter to you.
