  • What room are you considering, and what can you tell us about its dimensions?
  • Are there particular features or technology you want to explore?

What the project can include

The process begins with understanding the intended use and the space. From there, the simulator can be designed to address your requirements, including the technologies selected for the experience. The specific plan depends on your project, so it's useful to describe what you already have and what you hope the finished setup will do.

Before calling, consider preparing:

  • Approximate room measurements, if available
  • Photos or a simple sketch of the proposed room
  • Notes about how many people will use the system
  • A list of features you consider essential

One mistake to avoid is choosing equipment before confirming that the overall plan suits the room. Another is focusing only on play features while overlooking how you'll use the space day to day. A professional can help you discuss those decisions before the design is set.
